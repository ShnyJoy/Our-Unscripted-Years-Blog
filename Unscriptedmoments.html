This blog is about our journey as Junior High School students and the memories we made along the way. As we step into Senior High School, we look back on the people we met, the friendships we built, the challenges we faced, and the experiences that made our school years memorable. Through these stories, we hope to share a glimpse of what our Junior High School journey was like and how those years became an important part of our lives.

01
By Jedie

The Memories That Never Left

Some memories never really leave me, especially the ones I made during my Junior High School days. When I entered Grade 7, I was nervous because I didn't have any friends yet, and I was an introverted person. Then, two people came into my life, and we started hanging out together until we became a trio. We shared many funny moments, but we also had some misunderstandings. However, we always found a way to become friends again. I also experienced bullying, but my friends encouraged me to stand up for myself.

I also remember the first time I joined an intramural activity, which was running. I enjoyed it because it was fun and exciting. My Junior High School days were meaningful because I learned many lessons and experienced many happy moments with my friends and classmates. Those memories made my school life more memorable and unforgettable.

Even though we are now separated and have made new friends, I still remember them and the happy moments we shared. Sometimes, I wish we could be together again and bring back those memories. My Junior High School days will always be special to me because they gave me friendships and experiences that I will never forget.

02
By Samantha

My Unforgettable Journey

My Junior High School journey was full of happiness, challenges, and unforgettable memories. When I was in Grade 9, I met my OG friends. They are kind, loving, and caring people who have always been by my side through my ups and downs. Sometimes, after class, we went to Hagnaya Port just to relax, buy street food, and bond together. Whenever we had projects, we usually go to my friend's house to work on them together. Those simple moments made my school life more enjoyable.

One of my most memorable moments was our Junior High School graduation. It was a very special day because, after the ceremony, we went to San Remigio to celebrate and enjoy our time together. We also had a photobooth session to capture our happy memories. It was a day full of laughter and happiness that I will never forget.

During my school life, I learned many things from my teachers, classmates, and different subjects. I experienced challenges, especially when doing school activities and projects, but I always tried my best. Now that I am a Senior High School student, I realize how much I miss my Junior High School days. I will always treasure the memories, friendships, and experiences that made those years special.

By Junrey

My Journey as a Junior High Student

My Junior High School journey was very memorable. Even though I sometimes felt down because of our different personalities and there were times when I didn't like some of my classmates, we still created good memories together. Even during the earthquake at that time, we still managed to meet sometimes to pass our modules, spend a little time together, and go out for a while.

It was also a wonderful time because I had my own circle of friends: Rose, Marylove, Myca, Franco, Jojievana, and Ylainne. Even though we sometimes disagreed because of our pride, and there were times when I felt hurt or upset, we still stayed together. We shared laughter, jokes, and even our problems with one another. Sometimes we had misunderstandings, but those little moments made our friendship more special. Almost one year has passed, but our friendship remains strong. They have such a great vibe, and they understand me even without explaining everything. Being with them makes me feel comfortable.

My friends are also very supportive, and we always help each other whenever we can. They make my Junior High School journey more enjoyable. I am thankful for all the memories, the laughter, and experiences we shared because those moments taught me the value of friendship and staying together despite our differences. I will always cherish those memories because they are a special part of my Junior High School life.


04
By Shanaya

Through the Lens of Junior High

Looking back, Junior High was filled with different experiences, unexpected friendships, and memories that I will always treasure. At first, everything felt new and a little scary, but as time passed, I slowly found my place and began enjoying my school life. Throughout those years, I met a lot of people, but there were four who truly stuck with me. We eventually became very close and stayed together throughout the remaining years of Junior High School. One of my most memorable experiences was practicing for our musical theater and making a film about Noli Me Tangere during Grades 9 and 10. It was such a fun experience, especially because of the time we spent practicing and working together. We got to explore different places, shoot scenes, and bring our ideas to life. Another highlight was our Computer Science classes, where we got to code and build innovative things.

Junior High School may have started as an unfamiliar chapter, but it eventually became a place filled with people and moments that made those years special.

By Charise

Looking Back at My Junior High School Days

Looking back, my Junior High journey was a mix of nervous first days, small victories, and lessons that shaped who I am today. It wasn’t always easy, but every challenge helped me grow into a more confident person as I stepped into Senior High School.

My friends made those years truly unforgettable. We studied together during exams, laughed over silly inside jokes, and comforted each other when things got tough. I’ll never forget our school events, the funny moments in class, and even those embarrassing times that we now laugh about. My teachers were strict but caring. They taught me not just lessons from books, but also the value of hard work and kindness.

Now that I’m in Senior High, I realize how precious those years were. Junior High taught me that mistakes are just part of learning and that the best memories come from the people we meet along the way. I’m ready for this new chapter, but I’ll always look back on my Junior High days with a grateful heart.


06
By Jade

The Moments That Made It Special

Looking back on my Junior High School journey, I can say that it was truly unforgettable. At first, it was hard to begin a new chapter when I was in Grade 7. It was a roller-coaster journey filled with different experiences, challenges, and lessons, but along the way, I learned a lot and got to meet amazing people who made my journey memorable all the way until Grade 10. Some of the friends I made back in Grade 7 are still my friends today, even though they already transferred to other schools. Yet I'm still grateful because they were the ones who made my journey a little bit lighter and more enjoyable.

Some of the best memories I had during Junior High School were hanging out after class, having lunch with my friends, and laughing at our silly jokes. I also enjoyed group activities with my classmates, practice sessions, and random conversations. Those simple moments made my school days more fun and meaningful. There were times when we felt stressed because of schoolwork, deadlines, and grades, but we always found a way to enjoy our Junior High School journey despite the challenges.

Now that I'm already in Senior High School, I sometimes wish I could go back and experience those days again. I never realized how quickly time would pass. I may have moved on to a new chapter, but the memories, friendships, and lessons I gained during Junior High School will always be something I'll treasure. After all, it wasn't just about the school years; it was about the people who made those years special.


07
By Steve

The Memories We Carry

I have many good memories from Junior High School. I had many experiences, met many people, and learned many things that helped me develop as a person. Although the transition into school was not so easy at first, I soon got used to it and was able to enjoy everything.

Making friends and creating great memories with my classmates made my experience special. It was fun sharing jokes and laughter with my friends. However, there were also times when school became stressful because of activities, projects, and tests. There were also some funny and embarrassing moments! Taking part in school events, doing classroom activities, and being around people who made school enjoyable all contributed to my experience. Although some lessons were challenging, our teachers helped us learn and improve.

Now that I am in Senior High School, I realize how much I have changed since Junior High School. Although I have moved on to a new stage in my education, I will always carry these memories, friendships, and lessons with me. The main thing I learned from Junior High School is to make the most of every moment. Time passes quickly, and often, the simplest memories become the most special.
